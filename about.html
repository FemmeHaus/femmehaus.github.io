Our story

Why we built it.

FemmeHaus began life as Girls in the Hood — a Chicago community for women who wanted more from a night out. It has grown into a platform and media company, with the same people showing up.

FemmeHaus began with a simple belief: women deserve access — to resources, knowledge, community, and the rooms where culture and opportunity are being shaped.

Founder Sapphire Negussie created FemmeHaus to give women a resource where one may not have existed before: a space to feel safe, find belonging, build meaningful relationships, and gain the tools to progress personally and professionally.

That mission became the foundation for FemmeHaus Academy, our philanthropic and educational arm dedicated to investing in women’s wellbeing, growth and education.

And in many ways, FemmeHaus grew through the very type of connection it was created to foster. Through Chicago’s creative community, Sapphire met Asha Powell, who at the time was building her own path across creative direction, marketing and data analytics. Asha initially entered Sapphire’s world through modeling, but as they worked together, their relationship grew beyond the creative space — they discovered a shared vision for creating opportunities for women and building something with genuine purpose. Asha eventually joined FemmeHaus as a partner, bringing a complementary perspective and skill set to the platform.

As the community grew, so did the opportunities around it. FemmeHaus expanded from creating resources and safe spaces into producing experiences that place women in the rooms where culture is happening — from sports and entertainment to beauty, hospitality and lifestyle. Over time, brands began recognizing the value of authentically connecting with the community FemmeHaus had already built.

Today, FemmeHaus sits at the intersection of community, culture and opportunity — continuing to invest in women through FemmeHaus Academy, while creating experiences that give our community access to new rooms, relationships and possibilities.

We didn’t build a community around brands. We built a community around women, and grew into a platform where the right brands can meet them where they are.
